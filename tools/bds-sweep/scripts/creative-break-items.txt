# Which held items break blocks in creative, against BDS:
# `python tools/bds-sweep/script.py tools/bds-sweep/scripts/creative-break-items.txt --out DIR`.
# For each item, from the tick listed: clear the inventory, give the item
# (hotbar slot 0, held), put stone at (1,-59,0) and send StartDestroyBlock,
# then PredictDestroyBlock on the next input (as breaking-2.txt's creative
# scenarios). The start breaks the stone (destroy particles, LevelEvent
# 2001) unless the item cannot break blocks in creative.
#
# Arena as in breaking.txt: a stone floor at y=-61, the player at
# (0.5,-60,0.5) facing east (yaw -90) at the west face (4) of (1,-59,0).

26 /fill -4 -60 -4 4 -55 4 air
27 /gamemode creative @s
28 /clear @s
29 /tp @s 0.5 -60 0.5 -90 0
